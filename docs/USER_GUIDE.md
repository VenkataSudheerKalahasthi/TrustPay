# TrustPay – End User Guide (Client & Worker)

Welcome to **TrustPay**, the enterprise freelancing, digital contract, and escrow payment platform.

---

## 1. Getting Started

- **Registration & Role Selection**: Register as a **Client** (to post projects and hire talent) or a **Worker** (to find projects and deliver work).
- **Profile Verification**: Complete your professional profile, skills taxonomy, and avatar.

---

## 2. Digital Contracts & Escrow Payments

1. **Digital Contracts**: Create formal contracts specifying milestones, payment terms, and deliverables.
2. **SHA-256 Digital Signatures**: Review contract parameters and execute legally binding digital signatures.
3. **Escrow Protection**: Clients deposit milestone funds into escrow before work begins.
4. **Milestone Approval & Release**: Upon deliverable review, clients approve milestones to trigger automatic escrow fund transfers.

---

## 3. Realtime Collaboration & AI Assistant

- **Chat & Attachments**: Communicate via realtime WebSocket chat rooms with file attachments.
- **TrustPay AI Assistant**: Access the AI Workspace (`Ctrl+K` or `/ai`) powered by Google Gemini to:
  - Summarize complex contract clauses.
  - Extract deliverable action items.
  - Rewrite messages in a professional corporate tone.

---

## 4. Unified Global Search & Shortcuts

- **Global Search (`/search`)**: Search across Projects, Contracts, Workers, Messages, and Payments with instant suggestions and highlight matching tags (`<mark>`).
- **Command Palette (`Ctrl+K`)**: Use keyboard navigation (Up/Down arrows + Enter) to jump anywhere in the application.
- **Bookmarks & Pinned Items**: Pin frequently visited projects and contracts for 1-click access.

---

## 5. File Manager & Supabase Storage

- **File Manager (`/files`)**: Upload documents, store files in Supabase storage buckets, inspect SHA-256 checksums, and view time-limited signed download URLs.
- **Password-Protected Share Links**: Generate secure sharing links with custom passwords and expiration dates.
